## 2024-12-16 - Board scanning index overhead and loop invariant lookups in Chess logic
**Learning:** In recursive checkmate detection, AI minimax analysis, and check detection, piece scanning (e.g., `findKing`, `getAlivePieces`, `isUnderAttack`) is executed very frequently. Doing nested index-based lookups like `boxes[row][col]` or `getBox(row, col)` introduces redundant boundary checking and method dispatch overhead on every square. Additionally, looking up constant properties (like the target square or King square) inside a hot loop is a performance anti-pattern.
**Action:** Direct iteration over the board's 2D structure (`board.boxes`) eliminates custom bounds checks and array-indexing overhead entirely. Additionally, extracting invariant lookups (like `board.getBox(position.row, position.column)`) to local variables before hot loops significantly improves speed.
